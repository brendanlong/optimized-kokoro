# Provenance

The four lexicons here are `misaki/data/` from misaki 0.9.4 (the version Kokoro-FastAPI locks; installed by `reference/setup.sh`), rewritten in standard IPA by `tools/lexicon_to_ipa.py`: misaki writes them in Kokoro's one-letter symbols (`A` for e͡ɪ, `ʧ` for t͡ʃ), with `ɾ` and `ʔ` that kokoro reads as the English flap and `t`, and stress marks before the vowel; the script writes tie bars, `t̬`, `t` and syllable-start stress. The words and sounds are misaki's. misaki is © hexgrad and licensed Apache-2.0: https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki

They are compiled into the crate with `include_str!` and parsed when a `G2p` is created (about 40–50 ms per accent in a release build). Keys and phonemes borrow from the embedded text; only the case variants `Lexicon.grow_dictionary` adds are allocated.

`lexicon.rs` drops the keys "dr" and "mr" after loading them (`GUESSES`, `docs/divergences.md`, "misaki English: lowercase abbreviations").

## names.json

Generated by `tools/names/build.py`; don't edit it by hand. Each source is a module there:

- `tolkien.py`: the capitalized Sindarin, Quenya, Noldorin and Middle Quenya words (names, and proper nouns such as Maia) in [Eldamo](https://github.com/pfstrack/eldamo), Paul Strack's lexicon of Tolkien's languages (© 2015 Paul Strack, MIT; commit and hash pinned in the module), with the capitalized forms of them Tolkien wrote ("Valar", "Teleri"), pronounced by Tolkien's rules in Appendix E of *The Lord of the Rings*. The pronunciations are the script's, not Eldamo's.

Spellings English already uses (misaki's dictionaries, `exclude.tsv`) are left out; see the build script.

## names_skip.json

Also generated by `tools/names/build.py`: for Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese, the names their pipelines read as words because they're common words there ("Este" in Portuguese), from `tools/names/exclude_languages.tsv`. That list was picked by hand from the candidates `tools/names/common_words.py` finds in [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) 3.1.1's frequencies (code Apache-2.0, data CC BY-SA 4.0); only the reviewed spellings are committed, not wordfreq's data.
